I am **Jiwon Ryu**, an incoming **M.S. student in Computer Science at NYU Courant**.

I am interested in how embodied agents can **perceive, reason, and act** in the physical world. More specifically, I want to study how **perception, planning, and control** can be integrated to enable adaptive robot behavior in real-world environments, especially for manipulation.

My long-term goal is to contribute to robotic systems that can learn from experience and generalize across tasks rather than relying on narrow task-specific solutions. I am currently looking for a lab where I can deepen this research direction through work on **robot learning, embodied AI, and real-world manipulation**.

## Research Interests

- How can robots integrate **perception, planning, and control** to act adaptively in dynamic real-world environments?
- How can robot learning systems become more robust and generalizable across tasks, environments, and real-world failure cases?
